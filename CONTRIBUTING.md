# Contributing to ServerOS

Thanks for wanting to help. These are the defaults for every ServerOS repository that doesn't have its own `CONTRIBUTING.md`. The [panel](https://github.com/serveroshq/panel/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) has its own, more detailed guide.

- **Small fixes** (typos, docs, an obvious bug with a clear fix): open a pull request.
- **Anything bigger**: open an issue or a [discussion](https://github.com/serveroshq/panel/discussions) first and wait for a maintainer to agree on the approach.
- **Security problems**: never in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **One change per pull request**, with tests, and a description of what changed and how you tested it.
- **AI tools** are fine, but you're responsible for every line you submit. See the panel's [AI policy](https://github.com/serveroshq/panel/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).
- **Contributor License Agreement**: contributions to ServerOS projects are made under our [CLA](https://github.com/serveroshq/panel/blob/main/CLA.md). You keep the copyright in your work.

Everyone taking part agrees to our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
