# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Check the [docs](https://docs.serveros.com), then ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/serveroshq/panel/discussions) or on [Discord](https://discord.gg/serveros).
- **Something's broken in the code:** open an issue in the repo it belongs to, with steps to reproduce it.
- **Security problem:** don't post it publicly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Your serveros.com account, billing or ServerOS Cloud:** email [support@serveros.com](mailto:support@serveros.com).
